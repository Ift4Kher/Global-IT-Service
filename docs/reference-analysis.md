# Reference Website Analysis & Specification

## 1. Reference Inspection Summary
- **Reference URL**: `https://globalit.technology/services/`
- **Visual Design Reference**: High-resolution user screenshot (`input_file_0.png`)
- **Primary Objective**: Recreate the observable functionality, service categories, navigation, content hierarchy, and interactive features faithfully, while matching the supplied screenshot's corporate tech aesthetic.

## 2. Section-by-Section Specification

### A. Navigation & Header
- **Layout**: Fixed/Sticky top navigation bar with subtle blur/white background and border-bottom.
- **Brand Identity**: Stylized orbital technology emblem + "Global IT" logotype and "Technology for a Better Tomorrow" tagline.
- **Nav Links**:
  - `Services` (Active link with electric blue indicator bar / underline)
  - `Solutions` (Anchor link to services & solutions)
  - `About` (Anchor link to Why Choose Us / Company section)
  - `Contact` (Anchor link to Contact form)
- **Primary CTA**: "Talk to an Expert →" button with smooth hover scale and active states.
- **Mobile Menu**: Responsive hamburger toggle button with full keyboard navigation (Tab/Shift+Tab), ARIA expanded state, and Escape-key closure.

### B. Hero Section
- **Layout**: 2-column split layout on desktop, stacked on mobile/tablet.
- **Eyebrow**: "IT SERVICES & SOLUTIONS" in electric blue (`#0066FF`), uppercase, tracking-wider.
- **Headline (H1)**: "Technology That Moves Your Business Forward" (with "Forward" in electric blue).
- **Description**: "We provide managed IT services, cloud solutions, security and strategic support to help your business stay secure, efficient and ready for what's next."
- **Action CTAs**:
  - Primary: "Explore Services →" (Electric blue rounded button)
  - Secondary: "Contact Our Team" (White button with subtle border)
- **Trust Indicators Bar**:
  - `500+` Happy Clients (with team icon)
  - `99.9%` Uptime Guarantee (with shield-check icon)
  - `24/7` Expert Support (with customer service headset icon)
- **Right Hero Visual**: Laptop on wooden desk displaying glowing 3D holographic digital globe with cloud and shield nodes, overlooking panoramic cityscape through modern glass windows.
